One micro-confession for make.fun's "Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint" — exactly two parts: a "To:" line naming who it was meant for, and the unsent message itself, one line written exactly as it was typed, with no explanation after it. It is specific and honest, with a felt reason it stayed in drafts: the writer read her sister's diary the week before she left for university and has carried what she learned for four years, never finding a way to say "I know".

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
